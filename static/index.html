THE DIAGNOSIS
Angle Wrapping & Concentration

ZZFeatureMap encodes features into qubit rotation angles θ = 2x. Angles are 2π-periodic. Unbounded z-scores wrap repeatedly around the Bloch sphere, scrambling geometric distances. Additionally, reps=2 caused kernel concentration.

Why Scaling Matters in Quantum Machine Learning

Classical models like SVMs and Neural Networks easily absorb arbitrary feature scales via weight adjustments. But in gate-based quantum computing, input data controls unitary rotations: U(θ) = exp(-i θ Z / 2). Because quantum state space is naturally compact and trigonometric, features must match the coordinate geometry of Hilbert space. Tuning this encoding was the turning point of this research.

Research Scope & Defensible Findings

Quantum machine learning is frequently misrepresented with claims of instantaneous quantum supremacy. In this project, we maintained strict scientific honesty: classical SVM currently achieves 0.9939 ROC-AUC at 2.5ms latency, while our tuned QSVC achieves 0.9881 ROC-AUC at ~180ms on the identical matched 200-sample test cohort.

The real-world value of Q-FRAUDSHIELD lies in the hybrid dual-signal architecture: the quantum feature map provides an independent geometric perspective that flags subtle fraud cases missed by classical hyperplanes, allowing our LangGraph agent to protect financial institutions without reckless claims of quantum superiority.
